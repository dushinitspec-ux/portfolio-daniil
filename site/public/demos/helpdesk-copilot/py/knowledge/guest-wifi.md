# Guest Wi-Fi

The guest network **SO-Guest** is for visitors, customers and private devices of staff. It only
gives internet access: printers, file shares and the intranet are not reachable from it.

## Get the guest Wi-Fi password

The SO-Guest password changes every Monday at 06:00. The current password is shown on the
screen at reception and printed on the cards on the meeting room tables. Staff can also find it
on the intranet start page under "Guest Wi-Fi this week".

## Guest Wi-Fi for an event or a group

For workshops or more than 10 visitors, ask the IT desk at least two working days before the
event. IT can create a separate event network with its own password that stops working when
the event ends.

## Rules and limits

Guests are limited to 20 Mbit/s per device and are disconnected after 12 hours. Streaming and
file sharing sites may be blocked. Never give guests the SO-Staff password or your own account.
