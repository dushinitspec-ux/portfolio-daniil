# Office Wi-Fi

The staff network is **SO-Staff**. It is for company laptops and company phones only. Visitors
and private devices use the guest network (see "Guest Wi-Fi").

## Connect a company laptop

Company laptops connect to SO-Staff automatically after the first sign-in in the office. If the
laptop does not connect, click the Wi-Fi icon in the taskbar, choose **SO-Staff** and sign in
with your company email address (name.surname@sample-office.example) and your normal password.
Accept the certificate "radius.sample-office.example" if Windows asks.

## Connect a company phone

On a company phone, open the Wi-Fi settings, choose **SO-Staff**, set EAP method to PEAP and
enter your company email address and password. On iPhone, tap **Trust** when the certificate
appears. Private phones are not allowed on SO-Staff; use SO-Guest instead.

## Wi-Fi is slow or keeps disconnecting

1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, or restart the laptop.
2. In Wi-Fi settings choose **Forget** for SO-Staff and connect again.
3. Check whether colleagues next to you have the same problem. If everyone in one room is
   affected, an access point may be down: tell the IT desk (extension 100) which room.
4. Large downloads and video calls work better with a cable: every desk has a network port.

## Changed your password? Reconnect Wi-Fi

After a password change your laptop and phone keep trying the old password and can lock your
account. Open Wi-Fi settings, forget SO-Staff and connect again with the new password on every
device.
