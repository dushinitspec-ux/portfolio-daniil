# Teams calls and meeting rooms

Meetings use Microsoft Teams. The meeting room "Bosphorus" has a Teams room system with a
camera, a speaker bar and a touch panel on the table.

## Camera or microphone not working in Teams

1. In Teams click your profile picture, **Settings → Devices** and choose the right camera,
   microphone and speaker.
2. Close other apps that may use the camera (Zoom, the Camera app).
3. In Windows **Settings → Privacy & security → Camera / Microphone**, allow access for Teams.
4. Restart Teams. A headset plugged in after the call started may need a restart of the call.

## Start a meeting in the meeting room

Invite the room "Bosphorus" to the meeting in Outlook. The meeting then appears on the touch
panel in the room; tap **Join**. For an ad-hoc meeting tap **Meet** on the panel. Do not plug
your laptop into the room screen while the room system is in the call, or the sound will echo.

## Share your screen

In a call click **Share** and choose a window rather than the whole screen, so notifications
and other windows stay private. In the meeting room you can also share by clicking **Share**
from your laptop's Teams after joining the meeting with audio turned off.
