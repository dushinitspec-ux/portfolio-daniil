# Outlook and email problems

Email runs on Microsoft 365. Outlook on the web (outlook.office.com) always works when the
desktop app has a problem, so use it as a fallback.

## Outlook does not receive new email

1. Check the status bar at the bottom: "Working offline" means the **Send/Receive → Work
   Offline** button is on; click it again.
2. Check the Junk Email folder and your rules (**File → Manage Rules & Alerts**).
3. Sign in to outlook.office.com. If new email is there, restart the desktop app; if it is
   not, the sender's message has not reached us: ask the sender for the bounce message.

## Mailbox is full

Your mailbox holds 50 GB. Empty Deleted Items and Junk, and move old emails with large
attachments to OneDrive. Emails older than two years are moved to the online archive
automatically.

## Automatic replies when you are away

In Outlook go to **File → Automatic Replies** (in the new Outlook: **Settings → Account →
Automatic replies**), switch them on, set the start and end time and write one message for
colleagues and one for people outside the company.

## Email signature

Use the template on the intranet page "Email signature" so all signatures look the same. In
Outlook: **Settings → Account → Signatures**. Do not add images larger than 50 KB.
