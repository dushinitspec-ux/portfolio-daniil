# Accounts for new and leaving employees

IT creates and closes accounts only after a request from the manager or the office manager.

## New employee

The manager fills in the "New employee" form on the intranet at least five working days
before the first day: name, start date, job title, team, needed shared mailboxes and
software. IT prepares the account, the laptop and the badge. On the first day the new
colleague picks everything up at the IT desk at 09:30.

## Employee leaving the company

The manager fills in the "Leaving employee" form with the last working day. On that day at
18:00 IT blocks the account, converts the mailbox to a shared mailbox for the manager for
90 days, and collects the laptop, phone and badge.

## Access for a contractor or intern

Contractors and interns get a time-limited account that expires automatically. The manager
fills in the same "New employee" form and sets the end date.

## Change of name or role

For a new surname or a move to another team, the manager or the employee emails the IT desk.
The email address keeps working under the old name as well.
