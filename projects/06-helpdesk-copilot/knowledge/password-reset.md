# Passwords and account lockout

Your company account (name.surname@sample-office.example) is used for Windows, email, Teams,
Wi-Fi and the VPN. One password for all of them.

## Reset a forgotten password

Use the self-service password reset: go to the sign-in page of Microsoft 365, enter your
email address, click **Forgot my password** and confirm with your phone (Authenticator app or
SMS). The new password works everywhere within 5 minutes. Then update the password on your
phone's email app and reconnect the Wi-Fi.

## Account locked after wrong passwords

After 10 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes and then unlocks by
itself. If you are still locked out after 15 minutes, an old password is probably saved on a
phone or a second device and keeps locking the account: turn that device's Wi-Fi off and call
the IT desk on extension 100.

## Password rules

Use at least 14 characters. A short sentence of four or more words is easier to remember than
a complex word. Do not reuse a password from a private account. Passwords do not expire: you
only need to change it if you think someone else knows it.

## Change your password

On a company laptop press **Ctrl+Alt+Del → Change a password**. When working remotely,
connect the VPN first so the laptop learns the new password.
