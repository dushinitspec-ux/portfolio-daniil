# Reporting phishing and suspicious emails

Phishing emails try to make you click a link, open an attachment or enter your password.
Typical signs: urgency ("your account will be closed today"), an unexpected invoice or parcel
notice, a sender address that does not match the company name, or a request to pay or to buy
gift cards.

## Report a suspicious email

In Outlook select the email and click **Report → Report phishing** in the toolbar. The email
is sent to IT for review and removed from your inbox. Do not forward it to colleagues and do
not reply to it. If you are not sure, report it anyway: a false alarm costs nothing.

## You clicked a link or entered your password

Act immediately, every minute counts:

1. Change your password right away (see "Passwords and account lockout").
2. Call the IT desk on extension 100, do not just send an email. Tell IT what you clicked and
   when.
3. If you opened an attachment, disconnect the laptop from the network (unplug the cable and
   turn off Wi-Fi) but do not turn it off.

## Suspicious calls and messages

Phishing also comes by phone, SMS and WhatsApp. IT will never ask for your password or an MFA
code. If someone calling "from IT" or "from Microsoft" asks for it, hang up and report the
call to the IT desk.
