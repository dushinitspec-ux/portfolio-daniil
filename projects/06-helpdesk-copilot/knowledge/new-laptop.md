# New laptop setup

IT prepares every new laptop with Windows, Microsoft 365 apps and security software. You
receive it in a box with a charger and a sheet that has your first sign-in details.

## First sign-in

Connect the laptop to a network cable or to SO-Staff, sign in with your company email address
and the temporary password from the sheet, and choose a new password. Then set up Windows
Hello (PIN or fingerprint) and MFA when you are asked to.

## Get your files and apps

Sign in to OneDrive: your Desktop and Documents folders sync back from the cloud. Install the
other apps you need from the **Company Portal** app. Outlook and Teams are already installed.

## Docking station and second monitor

Connect the laptop to the docking station with the USB-C cable; the monitors, keyboard and
network cable on the desk work through the dock. If a monitor stays black, unplug the USB-C
cable, wait 10 seconds and plug it in again.

## Return the old laptop

Make sure OneDrive shows "up to date", then give the old laptop and its charger to the IT desk.
IT wipes it securely. Files saved outside OneDrive (for example in Downloads) are lost after
the wipe, so move them first.
