# Printers and scanning

There are two printers: **PRN-Floor1** (colour, next to the kitchen) and **PRN-Reception**
(black and white, behind reception). Both also scan and copy.

## Add a printer to your laptop

Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → Add device**. Wait until
PRN-Floor1 or PRN-Reception appears and click **Add device**. You must be in the office or
connected to the VPN. The printers are not available from the guest Wi-Fi.

## Print job is stuck in the queue

1. Open **Printers & scanners**, select the printer, **Open print queue**.
2. Cancel all your documents in the queue.
3. Turn the printer off and on with the power button, wait two minutes, print again.
4. If other people's jobs are stuck too, send the IT desk the printer name and the error
   shown on the printer display.

## Paper jam, toner and paper

The printer display shows where the paper is jammed; open only the flap it shows and pull the
paper out slowly. Spare toner and paper are in the cupboard under PRN-Floor1. When you use the
last toner cartridge, tell the office manager so a new one is ordered.

## Scan to email

Log in on the printer with your badge, choose **Scan → Send to me**. The scan arrives in your
mailbox as a PDF from scanner@sample-office.example within a minute. Check the Junk folder if
it does not arrive.
