# Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Every account needs a second factor in addition to the password. We use the **Microsoft
Authenticator** app. MFA is asked for when you sign in on a new device or from outside the
office.

## Set up MFA the first time

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone from the App Store or Google Play.
2. On your laptop open the "My Sign-ins" security info page (link on the intranet) and choose
   **Add sign-in method → Authenticator app**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app and approve the test notification.
4. Add your phone number as a backup method on the same page.

## New phone

Before you reset or give away the old phone, add the new phone in the security info page and
then delete the old one. If the old phone is already gone, call the IT desk: IT checks your
identity and issues a temporary access pass so you can register the new phone.

## Lost phone or no access to the app

Call the IT desk from any phone. IT can give you a temporary access pass that is valid for
8 hours. If the phone was lost or stolen, also follow "Lost or stolen device".

## MFA request you did not start

If your phone asks you to approve a sign-in that you did not start, press **Deny** and report
it immediately: it means someone else knows your password. Change your password right away
and tell the IT desk. This is a security incident.
