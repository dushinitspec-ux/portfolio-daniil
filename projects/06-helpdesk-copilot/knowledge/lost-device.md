# Lost or stolen device

A lost company laptop or phone can expose company data. Report it immediately, even if you
think it might turn up again.

## What to do first

1. Call the IT desk on extension 100 (outside office hours, the IT on-call phone on the
   intranet). Tell IT which device, when and where you last had it.
2. IT locks the device remotely and signs your account out on all devices. Laptops are
   encrypted, so the data cannot be read without your sign-in.
3. If it was stolen, report it to the police and send IT the report number.

## If the device is found again

Tell the IT desk before you use it. IT unlocks it or reinstalls it if it was wiped.

## Private phone with company email

If you lose a private phone that has the Outlook app with company email, report it the same
way. IT removes only the company data from the phone; your private data is not touched.
