## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in is from 14:00. Check-out is until 12:00.
- Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability. The guest must ask the
  reception; the concierge cannot confirm them.
- Luggage can be stored at the reception for free before check-in and after check-out.

## Breakfast and meals

- The hotel is all-inclusive.
- Breakfast: 07:00–10:00 in the Main Restaurant (ground floor).
- Late breakfast: 10:00–11:00 at the Pool Bar (coffee, pastries, fruit).
- Lunch: 12:30–14:30. Dinner: 19:00–21:30, both in the Main Restaurant.
- Vegetarian and gluten-free options are available at every meal. For allergies, tell
  the restaurant manager.
- Room service is available 24 hours and is not included in all-inclusive.

## Bars

- Pool Bar: 10:00–18:00. Lobby Bar: 10:00–00:00.
- Local drinks are included in all-inclusive. Imported drinks and fresh juice are paid.

## Wi-Fi

- Free Wi-Fi in all rooms and public areas.
- Network: LaraBreeze_Guest. The password is printed on the room key card holder.

## Pools and beach

- Outdoor pool: 08:00–19:00. Indoor heated pool: 08:00–20:00.
- Children's pool: 09:00–18:00.
- The private sandy beach is 150 m from the hotel, open 08:00–19:00.
- Sunbeds and umbrellas at the pool and on the beach are free.
- Beach towels: take them at the towel desk near the outdoor pool with your towel card.
  Change them there every day until 18:00.

## Spa and fitness

- Fitness centre: 07:00–21:00, free.
- Spa (hammam, sauna, steam room): 09:00–20:00, free for guests over 16.
- Massages and treatments are paid. Book them at the spa reception.

## Kids

- Kids club for ages 4–12: 10:00–12:30 and 14:30–17:30, free.
- Mini disco every evening at 20:30 at the amphitheatre.
- Baby cots are free; ask the reception.

## Transfers and transport

- Airport transfer (Antalya Airport, about 25 minutes) can be booked at the reception
  at least 24 hours in advance. The price depends on the car and number of people;
  the reception will confirm it.
- A taxi can be called by the reception at any time.
- The city bus stop is 300 m from the main entrance.

## Parking

- Free open-air parking for hotel guests. No reservation needed.

## Laundry

- Laundry and ironing service is paid. Leave the bag in the room and call the
  housekeeping desk before 10:00 to get it back the same day.

## Housekeeping

- Rooms are cleaned every day between 09:00 and 15:00.
- Towels are changed when you leave them on the floor.
- Use the "Do not disturb" sign if you do not want cleaning.

## Pets

- Pets are not allowed, except guide dogs.

## Smoking

- Smoking is not allowed in rooms or indoor areas. Smoking areas are marked near the
  lobby entrance and on the pool terrace.

## Safety and emergencies

- In an emergency, call the reception by dialling 0 from the room phone, any time.
- Turkey's emergency number is 112.
- The hotel doctor is available on call; the reception will arrange a visit.

## Payments

- Accepted: cash (TRY, EUR, USD) and Visa / Mastercard.
- Extra services can be charged to the room and paid at check-out.
